# Incomplete Search, Missing Sources, And Omissions

## Finding

The controller knew the DSAR was broad and required IT extraction, but the internal dossier still marks key sources as pending and the narrowed DSAR package omits important HR/legal documents that exist in the broader export.

## Evidence: Internal Dossier Says Data Source Inventory Is Pending

Evidence file:

- `Sharepoint\Thaer_DSAR_Dossier_With_HR_Details.docx`

Extracted lines:

```text
52: 3.2. Inventory of Data Sources (Pending IT/HR Input)
53: IT logs: Pending
54: 3.2.1. HR System Details (Updated)
55: Unit4 - Contains full personnel master data including salary, bank account details, employment terms, and HR history.
56: Teamtailor - Contains recruitment data including CV, cover letters, interview notes, and application metadata.
```

Why this matters:

- Your DSAR explicitly requested logs, audit trails, access records, Teams, email, tickets, and structured/unstructured datasets.
- The dossier only names Unit4 and Teamtailor, while IT logs are still pending.
- There is no visible final completion record showing those pending sources were later searched.

## Why This Is A Strong GDPR Point

The omission pattern supports the argument that the DSAR search was incomplete:

- The formal DSAR folder contains many work/project files but not the key HR/legal case documents.
- The broader export proves those documents existed.
- The internal dossier itself says IT logs were pending.
- The extension notice says specialized IT support was needed, proving Epical/Nordcloud knew relevant data was stored outside simple HR files.

## Complaint Wording

Epical/Nordcloud failed to conduct a complete and adequate search for personal data responsive to the Article 15 request. The internal dossier records pending IT/HR input and pending IT logs, while the apparent DSAR package omits obvious HR/legal case documents that the broader export proves were held by the controller.
