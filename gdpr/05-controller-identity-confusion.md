# Controller Identity Confusion: Nordcloud vs Epical

## Finding

The DSAR extension notice identifies the controller/processor context as `Nordcloud Hosting Sweden AB`, while employment and dismissal documents identify the employer as `Epical Sweden AB`. The evidence does not show a clear explanation of the legal relationship or which entity handled the DSAR as controller.

## Why This Is A Strong GDPR Point

Article 15 and Article 12 require transparent communication. A data subject must know who the controller is and who is responsible for the response.

The evidence creates these unresolved questions:

- Was the controller Nordcloud Hosting Sweden AB or Epical Sweden AB?
- If Nordcloud was renamed/acquired/restructured into Epical, where is that explained?
- If multiple controllers processed the data, why were their roles not identified?
- If Epical Sweden AB was the employer, why did the extension notice use Nordcloud Hosting Sweden AB?

## Complaint Wording

The controller failed to communicate transparently about controller identity. The DSAR extension notice identifies Nordcloud Hosting Sweden AB, while the relevant employment and dismissal documents identify Epical Sweden AB as the employer. The response package does not clearly explain the legal entity responsible for the DSAR, creating uncertainty about controller accountability.
