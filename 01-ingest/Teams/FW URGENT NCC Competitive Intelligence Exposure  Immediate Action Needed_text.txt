I have identified a critical business intelligence exposure at NCC linked to the current IFS Cloud and Azure Cosmos DB integration. This is not a standard IT security issue; it directly impacts NCC’s competitive position.

Key points, in brief:

- The current setup allows extraction of NCC’s operational and financial intelligence (“operational DNA”).
- Estimated exposure value: 130–1,050 MSEK annually if leveraged by competitors or external actors.
- Tender risk: Access to cost structures can give rivals a 0.5–3% bidding advantage on major tenders.
- Supplier leverage risk: Framework prices and preferred supplier data can be used to force margin erosion.
- Strategic IP risk: Execution data from large projects (e.g. data centers) provides competitors and hyperscalers with high-value benchmarks.
- Financial signaling risk: Project data reveals overruns and write-down risks months before public disclosure.
- Root cause: over-privileged consultant access, weak rollback logic, and audit-trail gaps, creating both data exfiltration and cover-up risk.

This requires immediate executive attention. I recommend a short, focused session with the NCC CIO or COO to outline:
1) the exposure,
2) the financial impact, and
3) concrete containment actions.
