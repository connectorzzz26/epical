Hi Team,







To support the launch of our DataOps, AI governance, and data masking offerings,Apart from the request to Epical, I have initiated formal GDPR Article 15 (Right of Access) requests across six organizations:







TCS  


IBM  


Nordcloud  


Volvo Cars  


Electrolux  


Crayon  







This is deliberate and strategic.







This is not a personal admin exercise. It is a real-world data maturity stress test. I am using my own identity and employment data as a traceable asset moving through enterprise data systems to expose where data architectures fail in practice.







The core idea is simple: treat identity as a data asset.  


If an organization cannot reliably link multiple corporate emails, reconcile records across regions or subsidiaries, or retrieve unstructured data such as chats and project material, then their data foundation is not ready for AI. GDPR forces them to prove
 this under legal pressure.







What we are testing:







Legacy Industrials (Volvo, Electrolux)  


Focus: dark data  


Strong structured data, weak control of unstructured and operational data  


Our play: make dark data visible, governed, and safe for AI use







Global Integrators and Enterprise Hubs (IBM, TCS)  


Focus: identity fragmentation  


Data split across geographies, domains, and delivery units  


Our play: federated governance and cross-entity identity unification







Cloud-Native Acquisition (Nordcloud, part of IBM)  


Focus: post-merger integration integrity  


Question: does data flow cleanly between subsidiary and parent, or does compliance break during M&A  


Our play: ensure data continuity, lineage, and compliance during integrations







Cloud Natives and Asset Managers (Crayon)  


Focus: metadata and lineage precision  


Risk: delivery speed outpaces governance  


Our play: automated metadata tagging, lineage tracking, and real-time masking







The outcome of this work will be a proprietary Epical Data Maturity Scorecard, benchmarking companies on clear criteria such as response time, completeness, data quality, identity consistency, and coverage of unstructured data.







This gives us a strong sales message:  


If a company cannot accurately fulfill a basic employee data request, their AI investments are already at risk.







I am acting as “Patient Zero” to surface systemic weaknesses in real enterprise data architectures. I will share findings as the 30-day statutory deadlines expire.






Next step:


Would you like me to create the Maturity Scorecard template now so we have
 clear KPIs ready when responses start arriving?







Please forward this to anyone who should be included in this discussion.








Best regards,  


Thaer Saidi
