## Scope note

These are internal architecture/use-case notes. They do not claim that Epical, Nordcloud, any customer, or any named third party has adopted Genesis Mesh unless their public proof artifacts exist and are explicitly referenced.

The strategic claim is narrower and safer:

> Genesis Mesh is the proposed governance/control-plane layer that would make the existing employee GDPR pipeline auditable, role-bound, revocable, and resistant to hidden influence loops.
