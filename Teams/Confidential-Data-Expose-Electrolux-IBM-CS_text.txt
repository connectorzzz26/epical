After reviewing Electrolux's privacy policy, I noticed a few areas that could be addressed in a message to the data privacy authorities:

Identification of Third Parties: While Electrolux mentions sharing data with group companies, service providers, suppliers, partners, and in case of corporate restructuring, the policy could be more specific, especially regarding the types of third parties.
Purpose of Sharing: The policy outlines various reasons for data sharing, including legal requirements, business operations, and marketing. However, it could further emphasize the legitimacy and necessity of each purpose.
Type of Data Shared: The policy details the types of personal data collected. It might be beneficial to explicitly state how the sensitivity of data is considered and ensure data minimization principles.
Control Measures: Electrolux states that third parties are expected to protect personal data in line with legal and contractual obligations. The policy could include more on oversight mechanisms and compliance standards.
User Rights and Choices: The policy addresses EU/EEA residents' rights regarding their data and provides options for marketing preferences. Further emphasizing user accessibility to exercise these rights could enhance clarity.
Updates and International Transfers: The policy mentions regular updates and international data transfers. Highlighting safeguards for these transfers, like Standard Contractual Clauses, could reinforce their commitment to data protection.
Consent and Withdrawal: The policy includes details on consent for data processing and its withdrawal. It could reinforce the ease of withdrawing consent and managing user preferences.
