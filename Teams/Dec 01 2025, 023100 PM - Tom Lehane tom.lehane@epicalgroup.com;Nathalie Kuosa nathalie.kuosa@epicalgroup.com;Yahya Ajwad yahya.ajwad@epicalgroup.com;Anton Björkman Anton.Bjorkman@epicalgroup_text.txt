Here is an example from our daily delivery work showing how we can benefit from AI without changing our ways of working. When setting up a new system, we can include
Azure Foundry platform alongside Azure Integration Services to support tasks such as:



		Faster delivery: speeding up complex mappings, generating test payloads, and reducing manual steps.

		Automatic documentation: producing API descriptions, examples, technical notes, and complete wiki pages from implementations.

		Reusable AI agents: a small fleet of agents that perform these tasks on demand: mapping support, test-data generation, documentation creation, and log analysis..




This makes the delivery smoother by offloading repetitive work.

The idea is to start enhancing our existing deliveries with AI while we work toward identifying tangible use cases that can later be part of our formal offering.
