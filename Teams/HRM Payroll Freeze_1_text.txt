Conclusion
The outage simulation confirmed that message flow recovery and resubmission work as intended, no evidence of message loss or timeout was observed.
However, the resubmission revealed multiple data related and mapping issues and inconsistent enrichment timing.
Key takeaways:
· The system recovers successfully after an outage without losing messages.
· Failures are mainly caused by HRM data validation and mapping inconsistencies.
· To ensure data accuracy after outages, improved error handling, and validation of enrichment timing are recommended before replay.
