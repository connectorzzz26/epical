Hi 
Everyone,


Quick note before the workshop in January.


Logistics



		Date/time: 13th, 10:00–15:00

		Location: meeting room in the co-working space next to our office in Gothenburg (online via Teams available (where it fits/works))

		Lunch: will be provided




 


Why we’re doing this


We want concrete, actionable ideas that support Epical’s direction (Nathalie/Märit will provide more input here on the day), with AI embedded in our ways of working and solutions towards custoemrs.


 


Workshop focus


We will focus on 2 main tracks


Please frame your ideas in one (or both) of these buckets:



		Innovate in delivery




Practical improvements in delivery methodology, automation, quality, speed, scalability—AI-supported where it makes sense.



		Scalable customer solutions (packaged offerings)




A repeatable, pre-made solution we can concept, deliver, and market consistently.


 


Format on the day



		We’ll work in small teams (2–3 people)

		The goal is not to talk about AI in general, but to produce specific proposals connected to ideas

		Each team will do a short pitch at the end: idea → feasibility → market/value → what’s needed to execute




 


Pre-work (please do before the 13th)



		Form a small team (2–3 people). Chat to your colleagues and try to form a team

		Start chatting with eachother and try to agree on:

		Are you 1 ”Innovate in delivery” or 2 ”Scalable customer solutions”?

		1–2 headline ideas you’d like to explore











		Bring one concrete idea you believe we can shape further during the workshop.

		Additional ideas should obviously not be discarded  In the backlog, we get back to them later






		For your idea, come prepared with a rough idea re.:

		What problem it solves (delivery/customer impact)

		Who benefits (internal teams / customers / both)

		What it takes to implement (time + tools + dependencies)

		Whether we might need external help









 


Reality & resourcing


We should be ambitious, but also honest about constraints. Anything beyond time+tools (delivery investment, external help, solution packaging) needs a clear case. If we spot a high-potential opportunity, we’ll shape it into a
business case after the workshop.

This is more of a Nathlie/Märit consideration.


Thanks, and looking forward to the day!
