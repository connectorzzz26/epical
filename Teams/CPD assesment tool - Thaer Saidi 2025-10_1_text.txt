			Career Path Development Framework 
			Competence
Level of proficiency, nature of assignments, and level of independence.	Interpersonal Skills 
Level of collaboration, communication, and feedback and conflict management. 

	Ownership & Initiative
Level of accountability, proactiveness/initiative, and innovation.
 
	Client & Business Impact 
Level of quality of deliverables, trust building, and value creation. 	Culture & Strategy alignment 
Level of alignment, and embodiment, with organizational virtues and strategy.
		Level 1
Junior	• Possesses basic proficiency in the role's core competencies
• Manages well defined tasks
• Requires guidance, gradually building confidence and independence	• Builds positive, professional relationships primarily with own team and mentor
• Communicates clearly and listens actively
• Incorporating feedback to support effective teamwork 	• Takes accountability for own tasks while seeking help when unsure
• Shows initiative and willingness to take on more responsibility as they grow in their role
• Contributes with a fresh perspective and offers improvement to existing processes 	• Ensures quality by supporting projects in a reliable manner under guidance
• Be prepared and organized in deliveries 
• Understands overall context and own contribution to value creation	• Seeks to understand the virtues and strategy
• Actively strives to embed the virtues and strategy in daily tasks 
		Level 2
Intermediate 	• Possesses solid proficiency in the role's core competencies
• Manages moderately complex tasks 
• Handling most tasks independently, seeking guidance for more complex challenges	• Contributes to a proactive collaboration that ensures alignment and progress
• Communicates across teams and cross-functional projects, adapting messages for diverse stakeholders 
• Gives and incorporates feedback to support performance and teamwork	• Delivers on commitment reliably, offering solutions and not only raising issues
•  Takes initiative and independently manages task and identifies improvements 
• Efficiently tweaks current processes or offers suggestions to creating new processes 	• Execute tasks with quality and meets stakeholders' expectations
• Demonstrates ability to contribute effectively
• Creates value through aligning deliverables with stakeholders' objectives	• Executes tasks with strategic goals and virtues in mind 
• Consistently demonstrating behaviors that reinforce company culture and strategy
		Level 3
Senior 	• Possesses advanced proficiency in the role's core competencies
• Manages high-impact, complex tasks
• Recognized resource for complex problem-solving, contributing strategically to projects and initiatives	• Fosters cross-functional partnerships, navigates complex stakeholder relationships
• Communicates effectively and actively shares knowledge to enhance collective performance
• Mentor junior colleagues, delivers in-depth feedback and manages conflicts 	• Takes full ownership of significant projects or processes, consistently delivering high-quality outcomes
• Takes proactive initiative to drive long-term sustainable improvements
• Suggests improvements and new ways of working for a long-term impact with competitive advantage	• Execute tasks with high-level quality and exceeds stakeholders' expectation
• Empowering teams and stakeholders through "thought leadership" 
• Advisor to client, challenging and adding value beyond initial requirements	• Align actions across levels guided by the organization's strategy and virtues 
• Serves as a virtue ambassador, modeling behaviors that strengthen culture and strategy alignment
		Level 4
Principal/
Managerial	• Possesses a broad and advanced proficiency within multiple domains
• Influences the organization's direction by shaping best practices and applying specialized knowledge to optimize outcomes
• Recognized key expert and strategic advisor, solving complex, cross-functional challenges 	• Demonstrates strong leadership, uniting and energizing teams around shared goals
• Excels in communication and team development in line with strategic objectives
• Mentors across levels and models best practices in feedback. Demonstrates strong conflict resolution skills	• Takes full accountability for results or business functions, ensuring alignment with strategic goals and deliver sustainable improvements
• Proactively drives long-term sustainable improvements for the business unit or project 
• Leads transformational change initiatives, fundamentally reshaping workflows, business models, or products	• Ensures high quality and strategic alignment through all levels
• Foster trusted partnerships, reinforcing the organization's reputation and strategic goals 
• Advices and challenges stakeholders or teams, ensuring strategic impact by shaping sustainable long-term solutions 	• Shapes the organization's direction, ensuring reflection of the organization's strategy and virtues
• Acts as a leading voice in reinforcing the strategic direction and embedding core virtues throughout the organization
