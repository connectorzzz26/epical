Regarding the single Track A environment: We can virtualize the SIT destination using Fabric.


Essentially, we route SIT traffic into a Fabric Lakehouse (simulating Track A) while keeping UAT traffic pointed at the real system. 


This gives us the strict isolation we need for UAT without blocking the developers in SIT.
