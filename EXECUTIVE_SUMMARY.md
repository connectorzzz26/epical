# Executive Summary — Product Owner Review

## 1. What this solution is

This repository is a structured solution for reviewing and preparing a response to an employee GDPR Data Subject Access Request (DSAR).

It takes exported enterprise data from systems such as Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, Exchange and HR/payroll platforms, then turns that material into:

- an indexed evidence inventory;
- GDPR findings;
- redacted evidence summaries;
- legal-ready deliverables;
- reusable document generators.

The current case is based on the Thaer Saidi / Epical / Nordcloud DSAR matter.

---

## 2. Why it matters

The solution helps answer four important questions:

1. What personal data exists?
2. Where does it come from?
3. Was the DSAR handled correctly?
4. What evidence is needed for legal or regulator review?

This is useful because DSAR cases can quickly become hard to manage when data is spread across email, Teams, SharePoint, HR systems, identity systems and operational logs.

---

## 3. How the repository is organised

The repository follows a clear five-stage flow:

1. `01-ingest/` — raw exports and extracted source material.
2. `02-index/` — inventory and classification.
3. `03-analysis/` — GDPR findings and compliance analysis.
4. `04-redacted-evidence/` — minimised evidence for review.
5. `05-deliverables/` — final legal / regulator-facing drafts.

This structure makes the work traceable from source material to final document.

---

## 4. Main deliverables

The key deliverables are:

- `05-deliverables/Letter_Before_Action_Thaer_Saidi.docx`
- `05-deliverables/Complaint_to_IMY_Thaer_Saidi.docx`
- `05-deliverables/findings_epical_lawyer_readiness.md`

The Word documents are generated by:

- `05-deliverables/generators/make_letter.py`
- `05-deliverables/generators/make_imy_complaint.py`

---

## 5. Main findings for review

The current analysis highlights these review areas:

- possible missed Article 15 DSAR deadline;
- unclear controller responsibility between Epical and Nordcloud;
- source inventory items still marked as pending;
- broad exclusions of mailboxes, Teams threads and attachments;
- possible exposure of sensitive third-party employee data;
- BankID identity and signature artifacts in the material;
- HR, payroll, absence and next-of-kin data in Unit4 exports;
- third-party health data in broad exports;
- operational credentials and Azure role data appearing together with personal data;
- possible need for GDPR breach assessment under Articles 33 and 34.

These are review findings. They should be validated against the source evidence before any external use.

---

## 6. Product Owner review focus

The Product Owner should focus on five questions:

1. Is the pipeline complete and understandable?
2. Are the findings traceable to evidence?
3. Is sensitive data handled safely?
4. Are the deliverables ready for counsel review?
5. Should the repository remain public, or should sensitive material be moved to a private evidence store?

---

## 7. Key risks

### Sensitive data risk

The repository may contain or reference sensitive personal data, including personnummer, salary data, BankID artifacts, HR records, family data and health-related information.

### Public repository risk

If this repository is public, there is a risk that confidential case material or personal data may be exposed.

### Legal wording risk

The documents are strong drafts, but they should not be sent externally without legal review.

### Evidence traceability risk

Every claim in the final documents should link back to a clear exhibit or source path.

---

## 8. Recommended decision

The solution is valuable and well structured, but it should be treated as a controlled case-handling framework, not as a public evidence dump.

Recommended decision:

> Approve the pipeline structure for review, but require evidence validation, redaction review, repository access review and counsel sign-off before any external use.

---

## 9. Recommended next steps

1. Freeze the current evidence snapshot.
2. Validate each finding against source evidence.
3. Move sensitive raw evidence to a private controlled location.
4. Add an evidence register with source path, issue, exhibit ID and redaction status.
5. Complete all placeholders in the generated documents.
6. Ask counsel to review the Letter Before Action and IMY complaint.
7. Decide whether a Swedish version of the IMY complaint is needed.
8. Add automated checks for personnummer, IBAN, BankID artifacts and other sensitive identifiers before commit.

---

## 10. Bottom line

This repository provides a clear DSAR / GDPR review pipeline and produces useful legal-ready drafts. The main value is traceability: it connects raw enterprise data to structured findings and formal deliverables.

Before external use, the priority is to verify the evidence, protect sensitive data and get legal review.
