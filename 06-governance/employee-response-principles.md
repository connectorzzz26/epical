# Employee Response Principles

1. The employee should understand what was searched and what was not searched.
2. The response should distinguish facts, interpretations, legal conclusions, and unresolved gaps.
3. Third-party data should be minimized without hiding the employee's own rights.
4. Redactions should be explained by category, not used as a black box.
5. The employee should be able to challenge omissions and disputed narratives.
6. Any automated processing should be disclosed in plain language where relevant.
7. The organization should not hide behind AI; a responsible authority must own the response.
8. The response should be humane, readable, and legally reviewable.
