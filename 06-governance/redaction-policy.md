# Redaction Policy

## Default rule

Circulate the minimum evidence needed to prove the point. Raw evidence remains inside the case workspace unless legal production requires otherwise.

## Mask by default

- Swedish personal identity numbers
- home addresses
- private phone numbers and emails
- salary, bank, IBAN, tax, and payroll information
- health, sick leave, and family/next-of-kin information
- credentials, tokens, secrets, IP allowlists, and internal security details
- customer/supplier confidential data
- third-party names where identity is not necessary
- signature artifacts and BankID details

## Preserve when necessary

- dates and sequence needed to prove DSAR timing
- role labels needed to explain processing responsibility
- source system names needed to prove coverage or omission
- redacted excerpts needed to prove over-disclosure or third-party exposure

## Redaction decision format

Each redaction decision should record:

- evidence item
- field or passage redacted
- reason
- GDPR principle
- reviewer status
- whether unredacted original is retained for confidential legal/regulator production
