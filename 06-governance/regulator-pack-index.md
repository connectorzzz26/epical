# Regulator Pack Index

If escalated, the governed case should be packaged as follows:

1. Executive chronology
2. Source coverage matrix
3. Article 15 completeness analysis
4. Evidence appendix, redacted
5. Disclosure decision log
6. Redaction policy
7. Processing register
8. Influence map and reviewer-feedback controls
9. Genesis Mesh case manifest
10. Agent role and authority model
11. Backer-service boundary statement
12. Revocation log
13. Unresolved gaps and requested controller action

## Regulator-facing thesis

The complaint is not only that specific data may have been omitted or over-disclosed. The governance issue is whether the controller can prove a complete, minimized, auditable, and influence-resistant employee DSAR process.
