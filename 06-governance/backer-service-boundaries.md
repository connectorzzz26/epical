# Backer Service Boundaries

## Principle

Initial Genesis Mesh backers can strengthen the compliance mesh, but they must not become uncontrolled processors of employee data.

## Allowed first

- trust-bundle validation
- revocation feed checks
- policy consistency review
- source coverage review from metadata
- redaction quality review on synthetic or minimized snippets
- secure packaging of final approved artifacts

## Prohibited by default

- raw HR/payroll data
- raw identity/personnummer/BankID material
- raw health or absence records
- unredacted Teams/SharePoint exports
- customer/supplier confidential content
- legal/counsel privileged drafts unless explicitly approved

## Treaty requirements

Every service use must define:

- sovereign id
- purpose
- data classes
- validity window
- output type
- revocation source
- audit requirements
- whether output can support final findings

## Demo posture

For demos, use redacted, minimized, or synthetic payloads. The value shown is the trust path and governance control, not exposure of raw private material.
