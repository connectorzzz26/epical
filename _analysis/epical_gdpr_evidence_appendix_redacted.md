# Draft strongest allegation wording

Based on the material, I allege that Epical/Nordcloud failed to handle my Article 15 DSAR in accordance with GDPR and, in doing so, processed and/or disclosed excessive third-party personal data. The most serious evidence is the presence of a 106-person employee/member spreadsheet containing personnummer, private contact details, addresses, salary and union/section indicators; BankID signature artifacts containing another person’s personal number, IP/device identifier and signature/certificate payload; and broad Teams/email exports containing third-party personal identifiers and health/sick-leave information. These facts indicate likely violations of GDPR Articles 5(1)(c), 5(1)(f), 9, 12, 15, 25 and 32, and potentially trigger breach-assessment duties under Articles 33/34.
