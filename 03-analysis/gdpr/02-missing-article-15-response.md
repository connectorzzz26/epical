## Why This Is A Strong GDPR Point

Article 15 is not satisfied merely by sending some files. The controller must provide:

- confirmation whether personal data is processed;
- access to the personal data;
- purposes of processing;
- categories of personal data;
- recipients or categories of recipients;
- retention period or criteria;
- rights to rectification, erasure, restriction, objection where applicable;
- right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority;
- source of the data where not collected from the data subject;
- automated decision-making/profiling information where applicable.

The local evidence shows some raw data was exported, but does not show the required explanatory Article 15 response.

## Complaint Wording

Epical/Nordcloud failed to provide a complete Article 15 response. The response package, as evidenced in the local export, lacks the mandatory processing information required by Article 15(1)(a)-(h), including purposes, categories, recipients, retention, source, rights, supervisory authority complaint information, and automated decision-making/profiling information.
